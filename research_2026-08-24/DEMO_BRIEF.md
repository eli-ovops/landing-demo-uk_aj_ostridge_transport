# Research-derived Demo brief — not authorised to build

**Status:** `BLOCKED — DEMO NOT READY`. No HTML/CSS/JS, images, deployment or actual Demo were made in this task.

## Design intent

A grounded, industrial transport-and-workshop concept should make the Maldon operator recognisable through its verified dual offer: road haulage plus commercial-vehicle/trailer repair. The visual language should be operational and direct—strong vehicle photography, compact capability groups, practical proof and a telephone/email contact close—rather than a generic logistics SaaS, booking site, dealership, or fashion-led template. Public branded lorry/workshop images are research references only; `CUSTOMER AUTHORIZATION: NOT VERIFIED`.

## One-page information architecture

1. **Hero:** business name, Maldon, “haulage and commercial vehicle repair” and one existing-contact CTA.
2. **Capability split:** (a) transport/haulage; (b) maintenance, inspections and servicing; (c) selected diagnostics/fabrication/trailer work. Every item must remain within the Business Fidelity Map.
3. **Operational reassurance:** compact, sourced signals—family operation, stated 25-year history, CPC-training claim—clearly limited to verified wording.
4. **Bottom 40%:** a report-attributed operating-context panel (without Monex marks or unsupported growth language), then location/direct contact. This adds new business information rather than repeating a generic CTA.
5. **Close:** repeat the same single direct route: call Alan’s published number or use a published business email. No form.

## Visual and mobile direction

- Use one dedicated, business-relevant Hero image; it must be rights-cleared or an honest non-factual original concept image if a future authorised Demo requires one.
- Avoid oversized outlined type and let the first mobile viewport show the business name, dual offer and CTA without forcing long line breaks.
- Group repair capabilities into scannable categories instead of an unstructured long list.
- Use a proof-led / image-led rhythm; the lower page earns its space with operating context and contact specificity, not a generic oversized CTA block.

## Shown in a future Demo (only after authorisation)
