## What the sample consistently does better

1. **One commercial promise first, then evidence.** Sites lead with an operational job (“keep fleet moving”, coverage or a logistics result), not a generic company introduction.
2. **Services are grouped around customer decisions.** Maintenance, compliance, repair/roadside and transport are distinct modules; long unstructured lists are avoided.
3. **Real operations create the proof layer.** Strong sites use actual fleet, workshop, people, named standards, current news or case studies—only where evidenced.
4. **The close is practical.** Location, call/email, availability/coverage and one clear next step replace a generic “contact us” block.
5. **Images are documentary, not decorative.** The strongest visual language is real vehicles, workshop process and people. This is the key gap in the present Demo candidate.
