## 4. Design Read + two directions

**Design Read:** A high-consideration local B2B “choose the job, then call” page for transport coordinators and commercial-vehicle operators—black fleet documentary photography, measured paper space, transport-red routing marks, and a grounded rather than luxury/tech visual voice.

`DESIGN_VARIANCE: 8` · `MOTION_INTENSITY: 6` · `VISUAL_DENSITY: 6`

### Direction E1 — In Motion / In Maintenance (SELECTED)

- **Hero:** the UI-clean real warehouse lorry occupies the entire opening viewport. Name, Maldon and the direct call route sit over the image as a low-contrast field annotation—not an offset paper card. The photo itself must identify heavy transport before the words do.
- **Rhythm:** full-screen working vehicle → a single full-bleed two-state business stage (`On the road` / `In the workshop`) with a distinct loaded-vehicle photograph → horizontal operating-facts ribbon → a two-image documentary proof sequence (yard / road scene) → portrait fleet picture that doubles as the final telephone close. The proof sequence is deliberately asymmetric and caption-light, not a card grid or carousel.
- **Bottom 40:** the documentary sequence establishes visible operational ownership before the factual 25-years/CPC/family-business notes and direct phone close. Emails/address sit as a compact footer line after the image, not a contact panel.
- **Purchase reason:** the difference is not a prettier hierarchy. It makes a visitor choose their operational context in one visual stage, then finishes with proof that the same business operates a visible fleet and a direct number to call—without navigating a long menu or decoding a list of repairs.

### Direction E2 — Workshop Floor (REJECTED)

- **Hero:** portrait fleet line-up cropped tall, with a narrow black contact rail; the vehicle line rather than warehouse interior is the dominant first impression.
- **Rhythm:** fleet proof → transport detail → maintenance capability as a compact workshop dossier → location/contact close.
- **Why rejected:** it is viable with the available real images, but it makes a prospective workshop caller wait too long for their route and resembles the earlier document-led direction more closely than E1.
